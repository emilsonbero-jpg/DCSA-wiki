Datamex College of Saint Adeline – Parañaque Campus is presented here as a branch of the Datamex Education System located along Dr. A. Santos Avenue in Sucat, Parañaque City. The institution’s story begins with the establishment of Datamex Computer School in 1978 under founder Oscar M. Ibay, followed by its elevation to college status in 1993 and subsequent expansion into Saint Adeline campuses.

History

The institution’s development, as outlined in this profile, spans vocational computer training, college-level programs, and the later establishment of Saint Adeline branches.

1978

Foundation and early years

Datamex Computer School was founded on October 28, 1978, by Oscar M. Ibay. The school is described as having received DECS approval and as providing vocational, computer-related training intended to prepare Filipinos for emerging technology work.

1993

Evolution to college

In 1993, the institution was elevated to Datamex Computer College. This transition marked an expansion beyond its original computer-school identity toward broader college-level academic offerings.

Later expansion

Establishment of Saint Adeline branches

The Datamex Education System later extended its network through Saint Adeline campuses. The Parañaque campus is associated with the Dr. A. Santos Avenue location in Sucat, serving students in the surrounding community.
